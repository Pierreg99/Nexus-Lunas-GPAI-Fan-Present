# Scope one freebie

Use this workflow when someone wants to create, improve, or resize a free gift, template, guide, or lightweight tool.

## Gather context

1. Read the creator rules (`freebie-creator-kit/RULES.md` in the full pack, or `RULES.md` inside the standalone creator kit) and the relevant audience hypothesis (`freebie-creator-kit/PROFILES.md` or standalone `PROFILES.md`) when present.
2. Ask for the idea, who it is for, the evidence for that need, available time/tools, permitted materials, intended format, and any delivery constraints. Ask only questions that could change scope or risk; otherwise state a clearly marked assumption.
3. Inspect supplied sources instead of assuming their contents. Never call an assumption a research finding.

## Define the brief

Produce a compact draft containing:

- One recipient in one situation, grounded in the available evidence.
- One task the recipient is trying to complete.
- One modest, honest outcome and a matching standalone deliverable.
- Version-one essentials and an explicit out-of-scope list.
- Time/tool constraints, dependencies, privacy/accessibility needs, and materials whose rights need checking.
- The riskiest assumption and the smallest low-cost, consent-respecting way to test it.
- Up to five observable acceptance checks.

Use plain language and a format a first-time recipient can follow. Prefer no signup, no unnecessary data, no external service dependency, and no recurring cost. Do not add a funnel, hidden upsell, analytics, or unrequested scope.

## Stop and hand off

Label evidence, assumptions, and open questions separately. Return the brief for human approval; do not begin production or claim that demand has been validated. If a request changes the audience, promise, rights, release channel, or costs materially, ask the human to resolve it before continuing.
