# Review a freebie

This is a read-only review. Read `freebie-creator-kit/RULES.md`, the approved brief, the relevant audience profile, and any stated acceptance checks. Inspect the actual draft and source files; do not infer that a feature works from a description alone.

## Review dimensions

Check only what is relevant to the format:

- Promise: is the promised outcome modest, true, and delivered by the actual files?
- First use: can the intended recipient find, open, and use the resource without unstated setup?
- Completeness: do the required files, local links, examples, and instructions exist and agree?
- Clarity and access: plain language, useful heading structure, descriptive links, keyboard use, contrast/readability, and narrow-screen use where applicable.
- Privacy and security: unnecessary data, external calls or tracking, secrets in files, unsafe handling of user input, and clear data-retention limits.
- Trust: clearly labeled illustrative content; no fabricated testimonials, metrics, affiliations, claims, permissions, or license.
- Upkeep and rights: dependencies, third-party assets, attribution, permissions, and any future service required to keep the freebie useful.

## Report findings

For each issue, provide severity (`blocker`, `fix soon`, or `optional`), exact evidence (file/section or reproduced behavior), recipient impact, smallest remedy, and a retest. Keep evidence and inference separate. Include checks you could not perform. Do not change files during review or say “approved”; the human makes the release decision. Return scope/promise conflicts to the human and maker before proceeding.

Finish with a short pass/fail checklist tied to the brief's acceptance checks. A clean review means no issues were found in the checks performed, not proof that every risk is absent.
